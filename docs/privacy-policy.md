# Personal Life Manager: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

Personal Life Manager ("the app") is a personal organiser for notes, tasks,
reminders, documents, shopping, budget, lending, medicines and personal
details. This policy explains what happens to your data. It matches the
Privacy page inside the app.

## Where your data is kept

- **On your phone**, in the app's private storage.
- **In your own Google Drive**, in a hidden app folder (`appDataFolder`).
  Only this app can open that folder, and it doesn't appear in the Drive app.
  Each change is saved there within seconds of making it. While you're
  offline it waits on the phone and is sent later.
- **Files you choose to copy to Drive folders** (for example
  "Personal Life Manager/Documents/…") are ordinary files in your Drive.

The app's makers run **no server**. We cannot see, collect, sell or share
your data, and nothing is sent anywhere except your own Google Drive.

## Google sign-in and permissions

You sign in with your own Google account. The app requests only:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, email address (`openid`, `email`, `profile`) | To show which account is signed in |
| `drive.appdata` | To save and sync your records in the hidden app folder |
| `drive.file` | To save copies and backups you choose into Drive folders, and only files the app created |

The app never sees the rest of your Drive, your Gmail, contacts or anything
else in your Google account. Use of Google data follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including its Limited Use requirements.

## Sync between phones

- Nothing is fetched automatically and nothing runs in the background.
- On another phone, tap **Sync** to get the latest. That includes reminders:
  a reminder added on one phone rings on another only after that phone syncs.
- If the same item was changed on two phones, **the change saved last is
  kept** and the other one is replaced.
- Keep your phone's **date & time and time zone set to automatic**. Reminders
  and sync rely on the correct time.

## Files

Documents, attachments and receipts up to **500 KB each** are saved to Drive
with their records. Photos are made smaller when taken.

## Security

- The app opens with your **phone's screen lock** (fingerprint, face, PIN or
  pattern). Anyone who knows your phone's PIN can open it.
- The app does **not encrypt** your data, on the phone or in Drive. Password
  notes are stored as typed. Your data is protected by your phone's screen lock
  and your Google account, so turn on Google 2-Step Verification.
- Sensitive screens block screenshots and screen recording.

## Signing out, uninstalling and deleting

- **Signing out** or **uninstalling** removes only the copy on that phone.
  Your data stays in your Google Drive and comes back when you sign in again.
- Before leaving you can **save a copy to your phone**: Account & sync →
  Save a copy to this phone. This creates a backup file that you keep.
- **Delete all my data** (Account & sync) deletes everything on the phone and
  the app's data in your Drive. You can also remove it in Google Drive →
  Settings → Manage apps → Personal Life Manager → Delete hidden app data.

## Analytics and advertising

None. The app has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
